# 📊 Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive sales dashboard built in Microsoft Excel that analyzes revenue, profit and performance across **17 European markets** for an outdoor and sports products business (Camping, Cycling, Fitness, Hiking, Travel).

![Dashboard Preview](dashboard.png)

---

## 🔑 Key KPIs

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | $123,724 |
| Gross Profit | $59,077 |
| Gross Profit Margin | 47.75% |
| Units Sold | 1,405 |
| Total Orders | 579 |
| COGS | $64,648 |

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Seasonality:** Revenue peaked in **June ($16K)** and stayed strong through summer; **February ($5K)** and **December ($7K)** were the weakest months.
- **Top markets:** United Kingdom (~$13.7K), Germany (~$13.1K), Spain (~$12K), Netherlands (~$11.3K), France (~$8.9K).
- **Sales channels:** Online Store **41%** ($50,972), Corporate 29% ($35,596), Retail Partners 17% ($21,114), Marketplace 13% ($16,042).
- **Regions:** Western Europe **33%** ($41,011), Central 27% ($33,280), Southern 21% ($25,763), Northern 19% ($23,671).

---

## 🧮 Pivot Tables Behind the Dashboard

1. **KPI Summary** – Revenue, Gross Profit, Margin %, Units Sold, Order Count, COGS
2. **Monthly Trend** – Revenue & Gross Profit by month
3. **Top 5 Countries** – Revenue with Top 5 filter
4. **Sales Channel** – Corporate, Marketplace, Online Store, Retail Partners
5. **Region-wise** – Central, Northern, Southern, Western Europe
6. **Country-wise** – Revenue across all 17 countries

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
- Slicers (Product Category, Country, Quarter) connected to all pivots
- KPI cards linked to pivot calculations
- Line, bar, column, pie and donut charts
- Navigation buttons between Dashboard, Pivot and Sales Data sheets

## ▶️ How to Use

1. Download `Sales_Dashboard.xlsx`
2. Open in **Microsoft Excel 2016 or later** (Excel 365 recommended)
3. Use the slicers on the right to filter by Product Category, Country or Quarter
4. Click **All Pivot** or **Sales Data** to view the underlying tables
